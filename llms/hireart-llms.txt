# HireArt

> HireArt is a workforce management platform that helps enterprises source, employ, and manage their contingent workforce - contract workers, freelancers, and staffing vendors - through a single self-serve system. Products include Employer of Record (EOR), on-demand sourcing and recruitment, a Vendor Management System (VMS), worker classification and compliance, program management, and freelancer management. HireArt does not currently publish a public developer API or API documentation.
